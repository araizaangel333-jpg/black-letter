## Exam template
1. Which branch acted, and under which clause does it claim power?
2. Is there a statute on point: authorizing, silent, or forbidding?
3. Which zone, and what follows for the burden of justification?
4. Which check could the other branches use, and did they?
5. Could a court even hear it (standing, political question)?

## Discussion questions from the schedule, answered for Mexico
Does your country divide powers? Yes, article 49, with the same three branches; the difference is the strength of the presidency in practice and the existence of autonomous constitutional organs (INE, Banxico, CNDH) that have no American equivalent inside the three-branch scheme.
