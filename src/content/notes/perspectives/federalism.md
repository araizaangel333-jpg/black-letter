## The one-paragraph answer to "why can Congress regulate a motel?"
Because the motel served interstate travelers and discrimination against Black travelers burdened interstate commerce; Congress may regulate activities with a substantial effect on commerce, and motive (here, morality and civil rights) does not matter as long as the commercial link is real. The Fourteenth Amendment could not do the job because the motel is a private actor.
