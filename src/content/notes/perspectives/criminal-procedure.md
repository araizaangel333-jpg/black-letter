## The three cases in one line each
Miranda: the station house is where the Fifth Amendment matters, so warn first. Duncan: the jury is fundamental, so the states must provide it for serious crimes. Roper: the Eighth Amendment grows with society, counted state by state, and juveniles may not be executed.
