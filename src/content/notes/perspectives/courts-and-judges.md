## Discussion answers for Mexico
How do people become judges? Until 2024, by judicial career and examinations for federal judges and appointment by the Senate for ministros; since the 2024 reform, by popular election. How is the Supreme Court composed? Nine ministros (reduced from eleven), elected, with a shorter term; it hears amparos, controversias and acciones, and its precedents now bind.
