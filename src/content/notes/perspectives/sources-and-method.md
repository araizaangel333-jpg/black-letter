## The ten-second map
Written law comes from legislatures and agencies at two levels, federal and state. Judges decide cases, and those decisions bind later judges below and beside them. Where no written law speaks, the judges' accumulated decisions are the law. The whole system is adversarial: the parties investigate and argue, the court decides.

## False friends to kill early
Jurisprudence is philosophy, not case law. Doctrine is a line of cases, not the opinion of scholars. Statute is a law, not bylaws. Common law is not derecho común. Sentence is only criminal.
