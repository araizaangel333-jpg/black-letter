## Lengths that work
For one element in a five-page memo: conclusion (1 sentence), rule (2–3), explanation (one paragraph per illustration, 4–6 sentences each), application (one paragraph), counter-analysis (3–5 sentences), conclusion (1). Roughly 300 to 450 words per element.

## The test for every sentence
Could the reader tell, from this sentence alone, whether it is rule, explanation or application? If a sentence mixes them (in Draker the plaintiff, like our client, …), split it.
