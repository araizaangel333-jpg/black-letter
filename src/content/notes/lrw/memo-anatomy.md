## The order to write it in
Discussion first (it is where the analysis lives), then Brief Answer, then Question Presented, then Facts (now you know which facts matter), then Conclusion, then the heading. Readers meet the parts in the opposite order, which is why the question and the answer must match the Discussion word for word.

## For the IIED memo due November 2
Threshold first: is IIED even available, or is the gravamen defamation or another tort (Zeltwanger, Draker)? Then the four Twyman elements in order: intent or recklessness (Standard Fruit), extreme and outrageous conduct (GTE Southwest for the pattern of conduct; Gonzales for conduct inside a marriage), causation, and severity (what evidence counts: therapy, diagnosis, corroborating witnesses). Write a counter-analysis for whichever element the other side can genuinely contest.

## Mexican contrast
A Mexican dictamen argues for the client and cites doctrine; the memo predicts for a colleague and cites cases with page numbers. The hardest habit to break is writing toward the conclusion instead of starting with it.
