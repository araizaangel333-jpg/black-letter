## A worked pattern (own words, from the course's cases)
Rule: IIED is a gap-filler that does not lie when the gravamen of the complaint is another tort, even one that fails. Illustration: in Draker, students posted a fake profile of a school official with false sexual content; she sued for defamation and IIED; the court affirmed summary judgment on IIED because the injury was to reputation, and the weakness of the defamation claim did not open the gap. Now the reader can see how the salon reviews will be treated before the application says so.
