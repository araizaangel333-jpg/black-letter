## How the professor tests it
Review Session #1 had two long fact patterns built entirely from offer and acceptance issues: a chain of letters and calls with price inquiries, quotations, counteroffers, revocations that crossed acceptances in the mail, and options. The issue outline gave a point for each issue spotted and each rule stated. Practice by drawing a time line of every communication with its legal effect, then writing one paragraph per communication.

## Mexican contrast
Mexico has no consideration requirement and makes an offer with a stated period irrevocable (CCF art. 1804); the mailbox rule is replaced by the reception theory (art. 1807). The habit to unlearn is assuming a promise is binding because it was made.
