## Mexican contrast
Caso fortuito and fuerza mayor (CCF art. 2111) excuse without fault, and some state codes adopt the teoría de la imprevisión that allows adjusting the contract; American law offers only excuse or enforcement, never judicial rewriting, which is why force majeure clauses matter so much in U.S. contracts.
