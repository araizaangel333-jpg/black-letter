## The exam template for a breach question
1. What did each party promise, and in what order was performance due?
2. Did the first performer perform, substantially perform, or materially fail?
3. If material: did the other party suspend, give time to cure, or terminate; was the termination itself a breach?
4. Any repudiation, assurance demand, retraction?
5. Then damages, with the limits.

## Mexican contrast
The excepción de contrato no cumplido and the pacto comisorio (CCF art. 1949) do the work of constructive conditions, with judicial rescission as the default and no formal concept of anticipatory repudiation.
