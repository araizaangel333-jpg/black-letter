## The professor's favorite fact pattern
An oral sale of goods with a mixed order (standard and custom items), a confirming letter, a cancellation letter, a delayed suit. Walk it in order: goods and merchants; writing signed by the party to be charged (his own cancellation letter admitting the order counts if it states a quantity); merchant's confirmation if both are merchants; specially manufactured goods for the custom part only (divisibility); admission; accrual and the four-year clock, with the repudiation question. Then write the counterarguments: the cancellation letter does not state a quantity; the buyer is no longer a merchant in that trade; the custom item was not substantially begun.
