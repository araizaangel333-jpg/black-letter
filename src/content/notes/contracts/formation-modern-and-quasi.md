## The three sources of obligation, side by side
Bargain (consideration): enforce the promise; expectation damages. Reliance (§ 90, § 87(2)): enforce the promise as justice requires; often reliance damages. Restitution (quasi-contract): no promise; pay the value of the benefit. The exam question is which source, and the answer determines the remedy.
