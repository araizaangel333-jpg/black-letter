## The arithmetic the exam will ask for
Write the formula, then plug in: contract price, market or cover price, costs saved, incidental costs, consequential losses that pass Hadley, minus what mitigation would have saved. Show every number's source in the facts; the issue outline gives points for the components, not only the total.

## Mexican contrast
Daños y perjuicios under CCF arts. 2108–2110 cover loss suffered and gain lost that are the immediate and direct consequence; the duty to mitigate is weak; penalty clauses are enforced up to the value of the obligation (art. 1843); and specific performance is the creditor's first choice (art. 1949). Four differences, each a potential exam trap for a Mexican lawyer.
