## The method the professor's interpretive guidance handout teaches, in my words
Start with the words; ask whether the dispute is about which meaning (ambiguity) or where the line falls (vagueness); list every source of meaning the parties can point to; rank them; assign the burden of proof; and only then decide. On the exam, show the ranking explicitly, because the points are in the method, not in the guess.

## Mexican contrast
CCF arts. 1851–1857 give a similar hierarchy (common intention over literal words, context, usage, contra proferentem), but without the parol evidence rule: Mexican courts freely hear evidence of prior negotiations.
