## For the Rapid Fire on your AI stack (due Oct. 29, 7:30 pm)
Name the tools you actually use (research, drafting, review, translation), what each is good for, what you never put into them, and how you verify. Two hundred to three hundred words; this site is itself an example of a tool built with AI under a no-copying policy, if you want a concrete one.

## Mexican contrast
Mexico has no ethics opinion of this kind yet; the Código de Ética profesional and the LFPDPPP supply the duties by analogy. The extra-credit prompt on AI around the world asks exactly this comparison.
