## The loyalty trap, in my words
The lab's fact patterns share a shape: someone senior asks for help with something that is good for them and bad for the company, or asks you to keep a secret from the board. The move is always the same: name the client, say what you can and cannot do for the person, and route the issue to the level that can decide.
