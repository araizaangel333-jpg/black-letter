## The shape of a governance essay
1. Hats: who is a shareholder, director, officer, contract party.
2. Powers: who can do the thing proposed (board resolution, shareholder vote, written consent, special meeting) and who cannot (the board cannot remove directors; the CEO cannot bind the board).
3. Duties: which duty each actor's conduct implicates (care, loyalty, oversight, good faith) and whether a conflict taints the vote.
4. Standard: business judgment rule or entire fairness; exculpation; reliance on reports.
5. Remedies: injunction, damages, removal, derivative or direct suit, and the practical advice (call the meeting, document the process, get independent advice, negotiate).

## Two 2025 questions worth an answer in class
What risks must a board oversee directly (cybersecurity, AI, financial reporting) and what may it delegate? Do the Texas reforms improve governance by cutting weak litigation, or weaken shareholder oversight by shielding management? Have one paragraph ready for each.

## Mexican contrast
The LMV borrowed the architecture (consejo, comités de auditoría y prácticas societarias, consejeros independientes, deberes de diligencia y lealtad), so the vocabulary will feel familiar. The difference is enforcement: derivative suits, Delaware case law and SEC actions make the duties bite in a way the acción de responsabilidad rarely does.
