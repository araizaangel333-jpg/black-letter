## Two questions the professor likes to ask
Should it be easier to pierce for tort creditors? (Most courts say yes in practice, and the policy reason is choice.) Should it be easier in the parent-subsidiary setting than against individuals? (The analysis is nominally the same, but studies suggest individuals get pierced more often than corporate parents.)

## Mexican contrast
Mexican law has only timid versions of this (desestimación de la personalidad jurídica, mostly in tax and labor matters). The reflex of a Mexican lawyer is to say the shareholders never answer; the American reflex is to list the factors and weigh them.
