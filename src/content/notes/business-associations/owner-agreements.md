## The pattern of the chapter 3 scenario
Several owners, some working and one investing, pick an entity and then nothing is written. Ten years later one is fired, one divorces in a community-property state, the company makes money but pays no dividends, and one dies leaving the interest to an heir nobody wants. Each event is a row in the checklist above; the exam answer walks the rows, says what the default rule does to each owner, and drafts the clause that would have prevented it.

## A way to remember it
Jobs, money, seats, votes, exits. If the agreement covers those five, deadlock and oppression rarely reach a courtroom.
