## Facts
DHB Industries (later Point Blank Solutions) made body armor for the military and police. Its CEO ran a multiyear accounting fraud and used company money for personal spending. The three outside directors formed the audit committee. The SEC alleged they were repeatedly warned of problems, failed to investigate, signed off on financial statements they had not reviewed, and sold stock at prices inflated by the fraud.

## Procedural posture
SEC civil complaints in federal court in Florida against the company and the former outside directors; the directors settled with civil penalties and officer-and-director bars (confirm the terms).

## Issue
Can outside directors face securities-law liability for fraud they did not commit but refused to see?

## Holding
As a matter of enforcement practice, yes: the SEC charged the directors personally for their willful blindness, and the casebook treats the case as a warning about the oversight function.

## Reasoning
Directors are the shareholders' monitor. An audit committee that ignores warnings and profits from the inflated stock has not merely been careless; it has abandoned the function that justifies its independence.

## Rule (own words)
If you sit on a board, read, ask and act when something smells wrong, or do not sit at all. Insurance will not cover what the law treats as fraud.
