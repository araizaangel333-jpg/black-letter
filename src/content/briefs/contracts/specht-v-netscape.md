## Facts
Users downloaded a free plug-in from a page with a download button; the license terms, including arbitration, appeared only if the user scrolled further down, and nothing required the user to see or click them. The users later sued over the software's data collection; the company invoked the arbitration clause.

## Procedural posture
Motion to compel arbitration denied; affirmed.

## Issue
Whether the users assented to license terms they were not required to view.

## Holding
No. A reasonably prudent user would not have known of the terms, so the click on download was not assent to them.

## Reasoning
Mutual assent online is judged by the same objective standard as on paper: the offeror must present the terms so that a reasonable user knows a bargain is proposed, and the user must do something that unambiguously signals agreement (a clickwrap I agree button meets both).

## Rule (own words)
If the terms are below the fold and the button says only download, there is no contract on those terms.
