## Facts
Wunder leased land from Groves to remove gravel and promised to leave it at a uniform grade. It took the best gravel and deliberately left the land uneven. Grading would cost about $60,000; the land graded would be worth about $12,000.

## Procedural posture
Award of diminution in value below; reversed; cost of completion awarded.

## Issue
Whether damages are the cost of the promised grading or the diminution in value.

## Holding
Cost of completion, because the breach was willful and the owner is entitled to what was promised.

## Reasoning
The court refused to let the breacher keep the money it saved by not performing; economic waste concerns yield where the breach is deliberate and the owner can use the money to do the work.

## Rule (own words)
Deliberately skip the work, pay for the work. Compare Peevyhouse, which answered the same facts the other way.
