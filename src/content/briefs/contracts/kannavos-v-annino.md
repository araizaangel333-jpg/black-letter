## Facts
The seller converted a single-family house into apartments without a permit, in violation of zoning, and advertised and sold it as a multi-family income property. The buyers learned of the violation when the city moved to enforce.

## Procedural posture
Suit to rescind; dismissed below; reversed.

## Issue
Whether the seller's silence about the zoning violation, after advertising the rental use, was actionable.

## Holding
Yes. Having represented the building as income property, the seller was bound to disclose the fact that made that representation misleading.

## Reasoning
Swinton protects silence; it does not protect half-truths. The buyers' failure to check the zoning did not bar relief, since the seller's statements invited reliance.

## Rule (own words)
Once you start talking about the thing, you have to tell the part that makes your words true.
