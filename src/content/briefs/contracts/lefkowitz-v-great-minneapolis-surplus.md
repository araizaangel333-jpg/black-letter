## Facts
The store advertised a black lapin stole worth $139.50 for $1, first come first served. Lefkowitz was first and tendered $1; the store refused, citing a house rule that the offer was for women only.

## Procedural posture
Judgment for Lefkowitz for $138.50; affirmed.

## Issue
Whether the ad was an offer and whether the store could add the women-only condition.

## Holding
Yes and no. The ad was definite enough to be an offer; the house rule was not in the ad and could not be added after acceptance.

## Reasoning
The test is whether the facts show some performance was promised in positive terms in return for something requested; first come first served, one item, one price, meets it.

## Rule (own words)
Make the ad specific enough and it is an offer; the terms are the ones you published, not the ones you keep behind the counter.
